Hello, I'm Cendekia! 👋

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Natural Language Processing at [Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)](https://mbzuai.ac.ae), supervised by [Prof. Kentaro Inui](https://kentaro-inui.github.io/). My research focuses on **model interpretability** and **multimodal AI**, with a central question:

**How do language, speech and vision-language models represent and process information internally, and how can we use that understanding to make them safer and more reliable?**

I am particularly interested in:

- **Mechanistic interpretability**:
  How do architectures such as Transformers and state-space models (e.g. Mamba) store, route and forget information?

- **Speech and multimodal models**:
  What do speech and vision-language models represent internally, and where does their safety behaviour live?

- **Low-resource languages**:
  How can we build robust language technology for Indonesian languages such as Sundanese and Javanese?

I received my **MSc in Natural Language Processing** from MBZUAI, with a thesis on vision-language model interpretability, and my **Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering** (cum laude) from [Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)](https://www.its.ac.id), Surabaya, Indonesia 🇮🇩. I was also a research intern in the Natural Language Understanding Team at [RIKEN AIP](https://aip.riken.jp/) in Japan 🇯🇵.
