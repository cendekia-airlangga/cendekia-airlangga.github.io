Hello, I'm Cendekia! 👋

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Natural Language Processing at [Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)](https://mbzuai.ac.ae), supervised by [Prof. Kentaro Inui](https://kentaro-inui.github.io/). My research sits at the intersection of **causality** and **interpretability**, with a central question:

**How can causality help us understand what language models compute, and how can we make language models better at understanding cause and effect?**

I am particularly interested in:

- **Interpreting models with causality**:
  Using causal tools such as interventions, causal mediation and causal abstraction to find which internal mechanisms actually drive a model's behaviour.

- **Causal understanding in language models**:
  Why do language models struggle with causal reasoning, and how can insights from interpretability help them reason better about cause and effect?

- **Low-resource languages**:
  How can we build robust language technology for Indonesian languages such as Sundanese and Javanese?

I received my **MSc in Natural Language Processing** from MBZUAI and my **Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering** (cum laude) from [Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)](https://www.its.ac.id), Surabaya, Indonesia 🇮🇩. I was also a research intern in the Natural Language Understanding Team at [RIKEN AIP](https://aip.riken.jp/) in Japan 🇯🇵.
